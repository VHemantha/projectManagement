# Investment entity (NZ)

An entity that holds investments rather than trades: investment company, family trust, or an
individual with a portfolio. (Rental properties held by it follow the residential rental rules.)

## What the accounts usually need

- **Bank and term deposits**: statements for every account, interest certificates showing gross
  interest and resident withholding tax (RWT).
- **Shares and funds**: broker or platform annual statements (holdings at year end, purchases,
  sales, dividends); dividend statements showing imputation credits and RWT.
- **PIE funds (e.g. KiwiSaver-type and managed funds)**: the annual tax statements; check the
  prescribed investor rate (PIR) used is right for the investor — 10.5%, 17.5% or 28%.
- **Foreign investments**: an individual whose foreign shares cost more than $50,000 in total may
  be in the foreign investment fund (FIF) rules — request the opening and closing values,
  purchases, sales and dividends for each holding; foreign tax paid. Flag the method for the
  preparer.
- **Crypto assets**: exchange statements of holdings and every disposal; flag for the preparer.
- **Loans**: statements showing interest and the closing balance, and what the money was used for.
- **Trusts**: trust deed changes, beneficiary distributions and resolutions, settlements and gifts,
  and the trust's assets and liabilities at year end. Trustee income is taxed at 39% from the
  2024-25 income year (with a $10,000 de minimis) — distributions matter. Trusts also have annual
  disclosure requirements (IR6) — request what is needed for them.
- **Companies**: dividends paid and imputation, shareholder current accounts.
- **Last year's lines**: every investment and income line in last year's accounts should appear
  again or be explained (sold, matured).

The preparer decides tax positions; ask for what they need and add a note.

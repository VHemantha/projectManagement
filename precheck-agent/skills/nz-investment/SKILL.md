# Investment entities (NZ)

Passive investment entities and portfolios, commonly managed by portfolio managers who report to
the client. Holdings may include foreign investments subject to the FIF rules, NZ shares and other
equity, debt instruments and other fixed-income vehicles, term deposits and crypto assets. The aim:
establish whether enough information has been supplied to prepare this year's financial statements
and tax returns. Identify the actual holdings, every portfolio manager, and any investment held
outside a managed portfolio. Apply each check according to relevance. (Rental properties held by
the entity follow the residential rental rules.)

## Core requirement: a complete year-end reporting package per managed portfolio

For each managed portfolio, the year-end reporting package for the right entity and financial year
must contain **both** the portfolio statement **and** the detailed income and tax summary — in one
report or separately. **A valuation report alone is not enough.** Read the report and confirm what
it contains: a file named "portfolio report" is not proof that everything required is there.

**Portfolio statement and movements:**
- the opening portfolio value and the closing valuation at the right dates;
- how the opening value moved to the closing value — additions, withdrawals, reinvested income,
  fees, valuation gains or losses, where relevant;
- detail enough to understand material movements and spot unexplained differences (a detailed
  reconciliation may be a separate statement).

**Income and tax summary:**
- income by source or investment, with supporting calculations or schedules;
- FIF calculations and their supporting information, where applicable;
- taxable income, PIE income and tax information, RWT deducted and imputation credits, where
  applicable.

Check the summary covers the same entity, portfolio and year as the valuation statement. Request
the missing section, period or calculation — not a duplicate complete package — and do not assume
every tax category applies.

## Coverage across all investments

Use last year's accounts, this year's records, the questionnaire and correspondence to identify
every manager and holding. A complete package from one manager does not mean all investments are
covered. For shares, deposits, debt instruments or crypto held directly or separately, identify the
provider statements, income records and transaction support needed; where unclear, ask the
accountant rather than assume a manager's report covers them.

## General checks that apply (use the general business rules for detail)

- **Bank accounts:** establish transaction coverage. Where the settings confirm Xero is complete
  for the year, do not request full transaction statements, but still obtain independent year-end
  balance evidence; otherwise request statements for the gaps.
- **Loans:** statements or annual summaries supporting the year's interest and the year-end
  balance of each loan; the purpose of new borrowing.
- **Deposits and other income:** deposit balances, interest, dividend support and tax information;
  trace funding of new deposits where needed. Do not request what the portfolio package already
  evidences adequately.
- **Expenses and accruals:** invoices and support for accrued expenses, including accounting fees.
- **IRD information and other records:** only where relevant, taking account of IRD access (see the
  settings) and what is already held internally.

## Output

- **Requests (section A, sent to the client):** concise and specific — the missing report,
  section, period, calculation or explanation, naming the manager, portfolio or investment.
  Priority "blocking" where the gap stops preparation.
- **Accountant questions and preparer notes (section B, internal):** report coverage, unexplained
  movements, differences, assumptions and uncertain accounting or tax matters, citing files, pages,
  periods and amounts.
- **Continuity:** keep the understanding of holdings, managers, income, tax schedules, questions
  and decisions. Answers the accountant gave earlier (M lines) are settled — do not ask again.
  Earlier requests (P lines) now satisfied are already provided. Accounting and tax conclusions
  remain the responsible accountant's.

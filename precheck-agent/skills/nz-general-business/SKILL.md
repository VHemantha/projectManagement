# General business (NZ)

A trading business: company, sole trader, partnership or look-through company. Last year's
accounts show what recurs; the questionnaire shows what changed.

## What the accounts usually need

- **Bank**: statements for every account (opened or closed in the year) covering the full year,
  and the year-end balance; credit cards and loans the same.
- **Revenue**: sales records or invoicing reports; if income is paid net (e.g. through an agency
  or platform), the gross statements.
- **GST**: if registered, the returns filed for the year and how they were filed (invoice or
  payments basis); the GST reconciliation. Registration is required above $60,000 of turnover in
  12 months — flag a business near or over it that is not registered.
- **Payroll**: if there are employees, payroll reports and employment information (payday filing),
  KiwiSaver, holiday pay and wages owing at year end.
- **Fixed assets**: invoices for assets bought, details of assets sold or scrapped; last year's
  asset register rolls forward. Assets under $1,000 can be written off; new assets bought from
  22 May 2025 may qualify for Investment Boost — flag for the preparer.
- **Stock**: a year-end stocktake with values, if stock is held.
- **Debtors and creditors**: year-end lists of amounts owed to and by the business; accrued
  expenses (e.g. accountancy fees) and prepayments carried from last year.
- **Loans, hire purchase and leases**: annual statements showing interest, repayments and the
  closing balance.
- **Shareholders and owners**: current account movements, salaries or drawings, money introduced,
  private expenses paid by the business. An overdrawn shareholder current account may attract
  interest or FBT — flag.
- **Motor vehicles**: logbook (a 90-day test period is valid for three years) or business-use
  details; this year's kilometres.
- **Home office**: area and costs, if claimed.
- **Entertainment**: details, as most is only 50% deductible.
- **Tax**: provisional tax paid and the IRD statement; ACC levies; prior-year tax payable.
- **Companies**: shareholder salaries and dividends declared (with imputation), Companies Office
  annual return details.

The preparer decides tax positions; ask for what they need and add a note.
